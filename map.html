    How To Use This Map

    Mapping Page Instructions

    This will be your main control page as you navigate through the different stops on the tour. Each number indicates the position you should be in to listen to each audio. For instance, number one is located in front of the Parroquia. Once you find your location, press number one on the map and you will be taken to the audio screen. Press the start arrow and the audio will begin. When you finish the first audio, press the back button near the upper left and return to the map. Find your position for second audio, tap number two. Continue in this manner throughout the tour.
